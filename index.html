Frequently Asked Questions

How can I start a crowdfunding campaign?

You can start a campaign by creating an account, providing your project details, setting a funding goal, and publishing it on our platform.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Donations may be tax-deductible depending on your country's regulations. Please consult a tax advisor for detailed guidance.

Can I donate anonymously?

Yes, you can choose to keep your donation anonymous while contributing to a campaign.

How will the funds be used?

All funds raised through campaigns are transferred directly to the project creator or designated organization. You can see a detailed breakdown on each campaign page.

Can I set up recurring donations?

Yes, you can choose to contribute to a campaign on a monthly basis or at a frequency you prefer.
